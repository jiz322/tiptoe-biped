## 3. Interpretation

- True IMU mounting offset ≈ −0.032 (08-18 crawl decomposition). The working
  trim is +0.12 past it: the policy must be told it leans ≈ 7° further back
  than it does. The trim is therefore a **plant-gap compensator, not IMU
  calibration**.
- Static CoM / mass model is **not** the lever: ≈ 0.3–0.37 kg unmodeled mass
  (W = 5.57 kg vs URDF 5.195) needs an 18 cm lever to shift CoM 1 cm; the
  knife-edge bounds |ΔCoM_x| ≲ 0.5–1 cm. Screws at the hip chassis cannot
  produce 7°. A mass-model fine-tune is not recommended.
- **Leading candidate: systematic knee joint-angle bias vs encoder** (belt
  slip / stretch / crawl-zero error). 12° knee × 0.2 m shank ≈ 4 cm foot
  displacement — the right magnitude, and tooth skips are observed.
  Independent check: Kinovea knee marker vs `sim_joint_pos`.
- Training-distribution holes found in `rl_env_cfg_p6.py` / `rl_env_p6.py`:
  `dr_com_offset_max = 0.015` is sampled **per link, per axis, independently**
  (`_dr_com_offset` shape `(num_reset, n_bodies, 3)`), so whole-body CoM_x std
  is ≈ 3–4 mm and never a coherent shift; `dr_mass_mult` 0.94–1.18 is
  location-less; `dr_proj_grav_bias_max = 0.10` is below the working +0.12.
